## T-CAT AUTOTECH — PREMIUM AUTOMOTIVE SERVICE WEBSITE

You are not being asked to generate a generic AI website.

You are acting as a **senior product designer, UX strategist, brand designer, conversion-focused copywriter, and expert frontend engineer** who has been hired to design the official digital experience for a professional automotive service company.

Your work must look and feel like it was designed and engineered by an experienced human digital agency — **not generated from a generic AI website template.**

The website is for:

# T-CAT ph / Thecheksons Autotech

A professional automotive repair, diagnostics, servicing, maintenance, electrical, hybrid, and electric vehicle service company based in Port Harcourt, Nigeria.

The website is currently being created as a **high-fidelity frontend prototype/pitch**.

Do NOT build a backend at this stage.

Do NOT create fake authentication, databases, payment systems, dashboards, admin panels, or real booking APIs.

Interactive elements such as forms, buttons, filters, navigation, and booking interfaces may be demonstrated visually, but they should clearly function as frontend prototype interactions unless a real integration has been provided.
